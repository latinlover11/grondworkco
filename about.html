Who We Are

Built by a crew that lives with the results.

Groundwork Outdoor Co. is a Longmont-based crew of hardscapers, fence builders, and lawn care pros who treat every property like it's our own.

Groundwork Outdoor Co. started with a simple idea: outdoor work should be done right the first time, by people who actually stand behind it. What began as a small crew building patios and fences in Longmont has grown into a full-service outdoor company serving Boulder County — without losing the hands-on approach we started with.

We don't subcontract out the trades we're known for. The same crew that pours footings for your fence is the one that grades your patio base and mows your lawn the following spring. That continuity is why our work holds its shape season after season.

Every project — big or small — gets the same walk-through, the same clear estimate, and the same crew that shows up when they say they will.
