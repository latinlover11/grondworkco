Who We Are

Built by a crew that lives with the results.

Waxwing Landscaping is a small Longmont crew of hardscapers, fence builders, and lawn care hands. We take the whole property — the structure underfoot and the planting around it — and we're still there after the build is finished.

The name comes from a bird. Waxwings arrive in a flock, strip a berry bush in an afternoon, and move on — a small crew that shows up together and finishes what it starts. We spent ten-plus years — and hundreds of jobs — building patios, walls, and fences across Boulder County before starting our own outfit, and that habit is the part we kept.

We don't subcontract out the trades we're known for. The crew that pours the footings for your fence is the crew that grades your patio base and mows the lawn the following spring. That continuity is why the work holds its shape season after season — same hands, same standard, whether it's a two-day repair or a full rebuild.

Every project gets the same walk-through, the same clear estimate, and the same people who said they'd be there. If something changes mid-build, you hear it from us before we do it, not after.
