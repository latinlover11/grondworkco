Stone-Edged Gravel Bed with Low-Water Plantings

A narrow strip along the brick foundation, edged in rough-cut stone, dressed with gravel, and set with junipers and boxwoods — built from gravel, edging stone, and plants the homeowner supplied.

Lafayette, CO
Client-Supplied Gravel, Edging & Plants
Edging, Gravel Bed & Plantings
October 2026

The Problem A Strip Too Narrow to Wing It

A bed this shallow between the brick and the lawn leaves no room for error. Turf creeps in at the edge, gravel spills out onto the grass, and anything planted has to clear the downspouts, the gas meter, and the foundation while still reading as deliberate from the street. The homeowner had already bought the gravel, the edging stone, and the plants. What the job needed was the layout and the setting that make those materials work as one line.

Design & Build Lay It Out, Set It, Dress It, Plant It

Four passes along the same strip:

  • 1. Layout: Marked the bed edge so the stone sweeps consistently from the fence corner to the front of the house.
  • 2. Stone Edging: Set rough-cut blocks on end along the cut line, tamped to grade with tight joints, so the curve holds its shape under a string trimmer.
  • 3. Gravel Bed: Dressed the bed in the homeowner’s gravel, worked tight to the brick and clear of the downspout shoes, so water leaves the wall instead of sitting against it.
  • 4. Plantings: Set junipers and boxwoods with room for their mature spread, kept back from the meter, the risers, and the window line.
  • 5. Service Access: Left the gas meter, hose bib, and downspouts clear and reachable — nothing planted over a fitting.

The Result A Line That Holds

The stone keeps the gravel off the lawn, the gravel keeps water off the brick, and the plantings sit clear of everything that has to stay accessible. The strip now reads as a finished bed rather than the gap between a wall and some grass — built from materials the homeowner chose and paid for, set where they actually work.

Photo Notes

All five photos are from the finished installation on 21 October. There is no before frame in this set: the bed was photographed after the edging, gravel, and plants were in. The lawn beyond the stone is fall-stressed in these frames, not damaged by the install.
