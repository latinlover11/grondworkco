Front Yard Lawn Stripping & 12-Ton River Rock Conversion

How we excavated patchy turf, established string-line subgrade levels, and installed 12 tons of river rock aggregate for a zero-mow front yard xeriscape in Longmont, CO.

Longmont, CO
12 Tons River Rock
Zero-Mow Xeriscape
2-Day Full Build

The Problem High Water Costs & Patchy Turf

An outdated front yard lawn suffered from heavy weed intrusion, compacted soil, high irrigation water bills, and uneven grading. The homeowner wanted to replace the high-maintenance lawn with a clean, drought-resilient xeriscape aggregate landscape that boosted curb appeal without needing constant watering or mowing.

Design & Solution Step-by-Step Execution

Our landscaping crew executed a complete turf-to-rock conversion process:

  • 1. Turf Stripping & Soil Excavation: Excavated patchy grass turf, weed root networks, and topsoil down to raw mineral earth across the front yard footprint.
  • 2. String-Line Laser Leveling: Set guide lines along the driveway border and sidewalk to micro-grade soil for uniform weight bearing and positive drainage away from the home foundation.
  • 3. 12-Ton River Rock Import & Transport: Hauled and wheelbarrowed 12 tons of premium river rock aggregate across the front yard layout.
  • 4. Screeding & Hand-Raking: Uniformly spread and raked stone to a 3"–4" depth, establishing clean borders around tree bases, mailboxes, and concrete aprons.

The Result Long-Lasting Curb Appeal

A striking, low-maintenance front yard xeriscape that slashes water usage, eliminates lawn mowing, and delivers long-lasting curb appeal.
