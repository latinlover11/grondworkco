Cedar Privacy Fence Installation with Custom Gate

A durable western red cedar boundary with a custom gate, carefully set to hold its line through Colorado weather.

Longmont, CO
Western Red Cedar
6 Feet Tall
2-Day Installation

The Problem Aging Fence Replacement

The existing fence was leaning, rotting at the base, and no longer provided privacy or security. The homeowner wanted a durable, attractive cedar fence that would withstand Colorado weather while enhancing their backyard.

Design & Solution Quality Cedar Fence Installation

Our crew removed the old fence and installed a new privacy fence using these steps:

  • 1. Old Fence Removal & Post Extraction: Carefully dismantled and removed the existing fence, including concrete footings and posts, to prepare for new installation.
  • 2. Pressure-Treated Post Setting: Set 4x4 pressure-treated posts in concrete footings at 8-foot intervals, ensuring proper depth and alignment for long-term stability.
  • 3. Western Red Cedar Board Installation: Installed premium western red cedar boards with proper spacing for ventilation and drainage, using stainless steel fasteners to prevent staining.
  • 4. Custom Gate Construction & Hardware: Built a matching cedar gate with heavy-duty hinges and latch, ensuring smooth operation and secure closure.
  • 5. Finishing & Detail Work: Applied protective sealant, trimmed excess material, and cleaned the work area for a professional finish.

The Result Beautiful, Durable Privacy Fence

The new western red cedar privacy fence provides excellent privacy, security, and aesthetic appeal. The custom gate operates smoothly, and the fence is built to last for years with minimal maintenance.
