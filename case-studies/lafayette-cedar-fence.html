The Challenge Setting Posts Along a Concrete Driveway & Walkway

The homeowner in Lafayette, CO needed a long-lasting property-line privacy fence and custom gates installed along the narrow concrete walkway and driveway separating neighboring homes. Setting structural fence posts directly where a solid concrete slab exists poses severe technical challenges: standard jackhammering creates jagged cracks and destabilizes neighboring slabs.

Engineering & Build Process Precision Concrete Sawing & Cedar Craftsmanship

Our team executed a multi-phase structural installation from initial concrete saw-cutting to final custom cedar joinery:

  • 1. Taut Stringline & Post Layout: Set exact property line string lines to center each post square with the driveway slope.
  • 2. Diamond Wet Saw Concrete Cutting: Cut clean 90° square relief openings through 4"+ fiber-reinforced concrete using heavy gas saws.
  • 3. Core Breakout & Sub-Frost Excavation: Removed concrete cores cleanly without cracking adjacent slabs and excavated footings below the frost line.
  • 4. High-Strength Concrete Setting: Set 4x4 pressure-treated posts plumb and true using Sakrete high-strength concrete mix.
  • 5. Western Red Cedar Framework & Gates: Crafted custom cedar framing, top cap rails, vertical pickets, and heavy-duty Z-frame gates.

The Result Clean Lines, Privacy, and Long-Term Stability

The finished western red cedar fence provides instant privacy between properties, secure gate access for vehicles and foot traffic, and sharp curb appeal with zero concrete slab damage.
