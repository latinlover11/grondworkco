Seasonal Landscape Clean-up & Invasive Weed Mitigation

How our crew extracted deep weed root networks, removed organic leaf litter, and re-aligned flagstone pathways around deck skirtings and stone borders in Longmont, CO.

Longmont, CO

Rock Beds & Flagstone Paths
Single-Day Refresh

The Problem Weed Overgrowth & Debris Accumulation

Late summer heat and rainfall caused rapid invasive weed growth across decorative river rock beds, flagstone walking paths, and turf borders. Weeds had sprouted around deck stairs and planter boxes, while accumulated leaf litter trapped moisture along house walls and fence lines.

The homeowner wanted a comprehensive seasonal clean-up to eliminate weeds down to the root, clear organic debris, and restore crisp hardscape borders.

Design & Solution Step-by-Step Restoration

Our landscape maintenance crew performed a detailed 4-step cleanup process:

  • 1. Complete Manual Root Grubbing: Hand-pulled invasive weed root systems out of river rock beds and flagstone joints without tearing the underlying weed barrier fabric.
  • 2. Debris & Leaf Clearance: Cleared trapped leaf litter and organic detritus from deck lattice, foundation perimeter walls, and planter tubs.
  • 3. Flagstone & Rock Bed Re-alignment: Re-seated shifted flagstone steppers, raked river rock level, and redefined turf borders for crisp geometric contrast.
  • 4. Surface Blow-Down & Final Polish: Power-blew all hardscapes, deck steps, and walkways, leaving a clean stone surface.

The Result Clean Landscape Contrast

Restored clean, manicured landscape contrast, eliminated weed competition around structural hardscapes, and refreshed the yard's overall aesthetic and usability.
