Wood fencing · Lakewood, Colorado

A quieter backyard with a clean privacy line.

Groundwork Landscaping built a roughly 60-foot, 6-foot-tall pressure-treated wood privacy fence and gate for a Lakewood home, creating a more private and finished backyard boundary.

Lakewood, CO
Approx. 60 linear feet
6 feet
Fence + 1 gate
The project

Privacy and separation, built to fit the property.

The Lakewood homeowner needed a new privacy boundary that would make the backyard feel more enclosed without looking out of place beside the home and neighboring yards. The project called for a straightforward, durable wood fence with a practical access gate.

Build details

  • Approx. 60-foot run: Laid out a continuous privacy line along the yard.
  • Six-foot height: Added meaningful separation from the neighboring properties.
  • Pressure-treated wood: Used exterior-rated lumber suited to Colorado’s dry sun, snow, and seasonal weather.
  • Single access gate: Built a matching gate with dark hardware for easy backyard access.

The result

The finished fence gives the yard a clear, cohesive edge and a more private place to use throughout the season. The natural wood tone also pairs well with the home’s brick exterior and surrounding gravel landscape.

Project snapshot

Completed: Mid-summer

Primary goal: Backyard privacy and separation

Material: Pressure-treated wood
