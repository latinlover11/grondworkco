Off-Street Vehicle Parking Pad & Ground Stabilization

How we excavated 4 inches of muddy dirt, corrected subgrade pitch, and installed 8 tons of compacted river rock aggregate for a heavy-capacity parking area in Longmont, CO.

Longmont, CO
8 Tons River Rock
4" Structural Bed
1 Day Completion

The Problem Site Challenge & Soil Degradation

An unpaved side-yard area on a residential property in Longmont suffered from severe vegetation overgrowth and rutted soil. Every spring snowmelt and summer rainstorm turned the raw dirt into deep mud, causing vehicles to track mud onto the driveway and risking negative slope drainage against the fence line.

The homeowner needed an off-street parking solution capable of bearing heavy vehicle loads without rutting, sinking, or requiring constant weeding.

Design & Solution Step-by-Step Construction

Our crew implemented a full subgrade preparation process before placing a single stone:

  • 1. Vegetation & Root Stripping: Grubbed out native weeds, grass clumps, and organic topsoil to expose firm, stable subgrade mineral soil across the entire parking footprint.
  • 2. Precision Subgrade Leveling: Re-graded and micro-pitched the ground to ensure uniform load distribution and positive surface runoff toward existing drainage channels.
  • 3. 8-Ton River Rock Delivery & Compaction: Transported, spread, and mechanically compacted 8 tons of heavy Colorado river rock aggregate, establishing a 4-inch deep permeable parking bed.
  • 4. Border Clean-off & Detail Blow-Down: Power-blew perimeter asphalt borders, fence lines, and deck skirts to leave a crisp, professional structural edge.

The Result Long-Term Impact

The completed river rock parking pad provides a clean, heavy-capacity off-street parking area that completely eliminates mud tracking and holds its shape under heavy vehicles season after season.
