Flagstone Steps, Sod & Mulch Bed Entry Refresh

A bare front entry rebuilt into a finished landscape — bedded flagstone steps, a curving stone path, fresh sod, planted and mulched beds with boulder accents.

Longmont, CO
Flagstone Steps & Path
New Sod, Plants & Mulch
Full Front-Entry Refresh

The Problem A Bare, Unfinished Entry

The porch sat a full step above raw, uneven ground with no safe way down, and the surrounding yard was bare soil and sparse grass. With no beds, no path, and no planting, the whole entry read unfinished — and every trip through the front door meant stepping off a concrete lip onto dirt.

Design & Solution Stone First, Then the Softscape

Our crew built the hardscape first, then layered in the lawn and planting so each piece lands on finished grade:

  • 1. Flagstone Step Footings: Prepared a compacted base at the porch and bedded large flagstone treads level and stable, closing the height gap safely.
  • 2. Bed Shaping & Grading: Shaped and graded the beds along the house and fence line so water moves away from the foundation and finished edges look intentional.
  • 3. Fresh Sod Installation: Unrolled new sod across the main lawn area, butted the seams tight, and rolled it for solid soil contact.
  • 4. Flagstone Path: Set a curving flagstone walkway from the steps out through the yard on a compacted base — no more tracking mud across the entry.
  • 5. Planting, Boulders & Mulch: Planted perennials, ornamental grasses, and shrubs, set boulder accents, and finished every bed with a clean layer of mulch.

The Result A Finished, Welcoming Entry

The entry now works and looks the part: solid flagstone steps and a path that keep feet dry, a thick new lawn along the fence, and layered, mulched planting beds that frame the house. It is a front yard that greets you — and one that only gets better as the planting fills in.
