# Door-hanger & leave-behind copy

Two formats: the classic door hanger (dropped 20 houses around every job site) and
a smaller counter drop for partner businesses (feed stores, brewpubs, realtors).
Print both on cardstock, single-sided hanger, business-card-size drop. Phone number
must be the biggest thing on the page after the headline.

---

## Door hanger — job-site neighbors

J-hook hanger, one job-site pass, not a mass mail drop. If a good before/after photo
of *that street's* job exists, put it on the hanger — local proof beats any headline.

> **Your neighbor's new yard**
>
> We just built [the fence / patio / landscape] at [street description — "the cedar
> fence down at the end of this street"]. Standalone cleanup: we left the site
> cleaner than we found it.
>
> **20 houses around every job get a free on-site estimate.**
> Hardscaping · Fencing · Lawn care · Xeriscaping
> Concrete, stone, and fence work built for Colorado freeze-thaw.
>
> **Call or text (720) 707-5411** · groundworkllc.netlify.app
> Free on-site estimates — Longmont & Boulder County

Body alternates by nearest service. Region honesty: batching to 20 homes, not
shots-in-the-dark — state it plainly (above line).

### Alternate top lines (rotate per batch — track which gets calls)

1. "Your neighbor's new yard" (default)
2. "That fence project you've been meaning to do?"
3. "We left this street more finished than we found it."
4. "Your neighbor just got their yard built. Reckon yours needs anything?"

**Never print something you can't substantiate** (no "$99 install" style offers).
The offer is the free estimate, which is true site-wide.

## Placement & tracking notes

- **When:** hang the 20-house ring within 48h of job completion, while the work is
  visible and the crew is remembered.
- **Where exactly:** front door, hanger over the knob — not mailboxes (federal no-no),
  not behind screen doors that don't open.
- **Log everything:** one line per batch in a notebook or sheet — street, date,
  calls within 14 days. Rotate top lines per batch; kill lines with zero calls.
- **Ask the source:** every caller gets "Where'd you hear about us?" logged too —
  hangers vs. GBP vs. social should all earn their keep independently.
- **Seasonality:** hangers are a warm-season tactic; spring fence/cleanup and fall
  aeration windows convert best. In winter, run the realtor list instead of doors.
