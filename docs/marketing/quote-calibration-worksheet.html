4Rules that keep quotes consistent

  • Same formula every yard. Never "it looked medium, so $X." If you can't fill rows 1–3 on this sheet, you're not quoting, you're guessing.
  • Round to $25. Odd numbers read like math; round ones read like trust.
  • Never go below your floor rate. Your rate ÷ hour is written once in Row 2 — if a yard works out below it after friction, the price is the price.
  • Quote separate line items only when asked: cleanup, blowout, and hauling are one job until the customer splits them.
  • Practice-run quotes don't go out. This first sheet calibrates you — send nothing until 2 practice runs land within 10% of each other for similar yards.
