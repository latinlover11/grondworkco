Before the first freeze

Front Range winter doesn't wait for your calendar — the first hard freeze usually lands by mid-October, and some years it comes early. Two things do not forgive it: leaves smothering your lawn, and water still sitting in your sprinkler lines.

What freeze actually costs a sprinkler system: the backflow preventer sits above ground where the cold hits first — when the water inside it freezes, it splits. Come spring that's a plumber visit, not a fix-it-yourself. Twenty minutes of compressed air in the fall is the whole difference.
