# The Fall Reset — campaign kit

Theme for a book-it-now push through early November. Everything funnels to the
new landing page: `groundworkllc.netlify.app/fall-cleanup-longmont.html` (short link
in captions: just the phone number — (720) 707-5411).

**All packages are BY QUOTE now** — no dollar figures on the page, the flyer, or in
any post. Every package starts with the free on-site walk-through; the quote is
your yard's number. Keep your own internal pacing notes (below) so quotes stay
consistent and profitable from house to house.

## The offer (as it stands on the page)

| Package | Pricing | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **The Fall Reset** | By quote (free walk-through) | Leaf cleanup lawn+beds (hauled), final mow + re-edge, aeration + overseed, perennial cut-back |
| **The Total Winterize** | By quote (free walk-through) | Reset + sprinkler blowout (≤8 zones), gutter clearing (single-story), frost walk-through checklist |
| Add-ons | Quoted with the job | Pot/window-box cleanout · fence & gate winter check · mulch top-up |
| **Snow shoveling pilot** | Per-visit, set at signup | ≤24h after ≥2" snow; limited homes; route-checked at booking |

Structure logic: the Reset is the low-thought "yes" (pure lawn care, no weather
deadline on equipment), the Winterize is the margin + fear-of-pipes upgrade, and
the pilot turns winter dead time into a bookable service with almost zero routes
committed — you can still say no to any signup.

### Sensible quote-calibration checks (do once, this week)

- [ ] One full Reset on your worst nearby yard: time it. Work out your effective
  hourly rate *plus* haul costs at the size you quoted — that number is your
  stopwatch for quoting the next 20 yards, and the floor for anything smaller.
- [ ] Blowout: confirm you have compressor/kit rated for a residential system, and
  add a per-zone surcharge past 8.
- [ ] Gutters: decide the height cutoff (single-story only — that on the page is
  deliberate) and what you add for second stories when asked.
- [ ] Snow pilot: pick the flat per-visit number you can live with all winter,
  state it at signup, and cap the number of homes (10?).

## Print files and build wiring

Four print pieces are HTML sources with PDFs generated next to them in `docs/marketing/`:

- `flyer-door-hanger.html` / `.pdf`
- `flyer-fall-cleanup.html` / `.pdf`
- `realtor-counter-card.html` / `.pdf`
- `quote-calibration-worksheet.html` / `.pdf`

`python3 build.py` regenerates a PDF only when its HTML source is newer than the PDF. The list lives in `PDF_JOBS` in `build.py`. If no PDF library is available (weasyprint, or the `/tmp/pdfgen` venv), the build prints a skip notice and still succeeds, so the site deploy is never blocked.

`docs/` is excluded from the site build, so the print sources never ship. The site output stays at 31 HTML pages.
