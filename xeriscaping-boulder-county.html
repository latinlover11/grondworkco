Xeriscaping is more than replacing grass with rock. We plan transitions, drainage, access, focal points, and maintenance so the finished yard feels intentional rather than barren.

Gravel conversions

Durable gravel beds and clean edges for areas where turf is struggling or maintenance is too high.

Low-water planting

Practical planting zones that complement the hardscape and reduce irrigation demands.

Our Boulder County xeriscape process

  1. Site assessment: We review sun, slope, drainage, existing lawn, access, and how you use the yard.
  2. Material plan: We define gravel, stone, edging, planting, and any grading or removal required.
  3. Site preparation: We strip, grade, install the base, and build the landscape in durable layers.
  4. Finished guidance: We explain how to care for the new landscape as it settles in.

Lawn replacement rebates can cover a real share of your conversion.

Resource Central’s Lawn Replacement program partners with city water providers — including the City of Longmont — to subsidize turf removal and water-wise conversion for homeowners. Depending on the program year and provider, that can mean paying only a reduced per-square-foot share for a professional conversion.

We build the yard side of it: design, turf removal, grading, gravel and stone installation, and low-water planting zones. You apply directly with the program and we scope the work to fit its guidelines.

Program eligibility

Applications open seasonally and funding is limited — homeowners apply through Resource Central first, then installation follows their schedule.

What we scope

Turf removal and grading to program specs, gravel base and edging, stone features, and water-wise planting that stays within finished requirements.
