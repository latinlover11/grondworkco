Our Boulder County xeriscape process

  1. Site assessment: We review sun, slope, drainage, existing lawn, access, and how you use the yard.
  2. Material plan: We define gravel, stone, edging, planting, and any grading or removal required.
  3. Site preparation: We strip, grade, install the base, and build the landscape in durable layers.
  4. Finished guidance: We explain how to care for the new landscape as it settles in.
