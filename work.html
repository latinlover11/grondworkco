Case Study #7 · Hardscaping & Landscape Installation

Flagstone Steps, Sod & Mulch Bed Entry Refresh

Location: Longmont, CO Hardscape: Flagstone Steps & Path Softscape: Sod, Plants & Mulch
⚠ The Problem

A porch a full step above raw, uneven ground with no safe way down — bare soil, no path, and no planting anywhere around the front entry.

🔨 The Build
  • Flagstone Steps: Bedded large treads on a compacted base to close the porch height gap safely.
  • Curving Path: Set a flagstone walkway from the steps out through the yard.
  • New Sod: Unrolled, butted, and rolled fresh sod for the main lawn.
  • Beds & Boulders: Planted grasses, perennials, and shrubs, then mulched with boulder accents.
✓ The Result

A finished, welcoming front entry — safe stone access, a thick new lawn, and layered planting beds that frame the house.

Case Study #5 · Natural Stone & Xeriscaping

Vertical Flagstone Monolith & Dual-Gravel Xeriscape

Location: Longmont, CO Stone: Standing Red Colorado Sandstone Scope: Sod Strip & Dual-Rock Bed Layout
⚠ The Problem

A shady backyard with patchy, weed-infested turfgrass that wasted hundreds of gallons of water and required constant mowing without delivering aesthetic value.

🔨 Design & Execution
  • Sod Stripping & Tilling: Excavated weed-infested sod down to subgrade earth.
  • Steel Ring Edging: Installed circular steel rings around tree & feature bed.
  • Monolith Footing Dig: Hand-dug deep footings to set standing flagstone slabs.
  • Dual-Color Aggregate: Laid red granite breeze rings over charcoal gravel.
✓ The Result

A striking, zero-mow backyard garden oasis with vertical stone monoliths, dwarf blue spruce plantings, and high-contrast stone borders.

Case Study #4 · Woodwork & Concrete Coring

Custom Cedar Property-Line Fence & Gate in Lafayette

Location: Lafayette, CO Material: Western Red Cedar & Concrete Scope: Saw-Coring, Footings, Fence & Gates
⚠ The Problem

Setting property-line fence posts directly where a solid concrete walkway and driveway exist. Standard jackhammering alone risks shattering surrounding slabs and creating uneven jagged edges.

🔨 Design & Execution
  • Diamond Saw-Cutting: Cut precise 90° relief square holes through 4"+ concrete.
  • Core Breakout & Footings: Removed concrete cores & excavated sub-frost footings.
  • Post Setting: Set 4x4 pressure-treated posts in high-strength concrete.
  • Cedar Carpentry: Built custom cedar framing, cap rails, and double gates.
✓ The Result

A beautiful, durable western red cedar property-line fence with custom gates, zero slab cracking, and long-lasting structural stability.

Case Study #1 · Landscaping & Excavation

Off-Street Vehicle Parking Pad & Ground Stabilization

Location: Longmont, CO Material: 8 Tons River Rock Scope: Clearing, Leveling & Aggregate
⚠ The Problem

An unpaved side-yard area was overgrown with deep-rooted weeds and rutted dirt. Parking vehicles on raw earth caused severe mud pooling during snowmelts, washing dirt onto the driveway and creating an unkempt property eye-sore.

🔨 Design & Execution
  • Vegetation Stripping: Grubbed out weeds & topsoil to expose subgrade.
  • Precision Leveling: Re-graded terrain to eliminate dips and ensure runoff.
  • 8-Ton Aggregate Lay: Compacted 8 tons of river rock to a 4" depth.
  • Perimeter Clean-off: Power-blew asphalt borders for a sharp edge.
✓ The Result

A heavy-capacity, low-maintenance parking pad that completely eliminates mud tracking, holds its shape under heavy vehicles, and cleanly integrates into the yard.

Case Study #2 · Seasonal Care & Landscape Restoration

Seasonal Landscape Clean-up & Invasive Weed Mitigation

Location: Longmont, CO Scope: Weed Extraction & Leaf Clearance Area: Rock Beds & Walkways

Case Study #3 · Xeriscaping & Turf Conversion

Front Yard Lawn Stripping & 12-Ton River Rock Conversion

Location: Longmont, CO Material: 12 Tons River Rock Aggregate Scope: Turf Removal & Rock Spread
⚠ The Problem

An outdated front yard lawn suffered from patchy turf, heavy weed intrusion, high irrigation demands, and uneven grading. The homeowner desired a clean, water-wise xeriscape conversion that eliminated constant mowing.

🔨 Design & Execution
  • Turf Stripping: Excavated patchy turf down to clean subgrade earth.
  • String-Line Leveling: Set guide lines along driveway border to micro-grade soil.
  • 12-Ton Rock Import: Hauled and wheelbarrowed 12 tons of river rock.
  • Screeding & Raking: Raked stone to a 3"–4" depth for clean borders.
✓ The Result

A striking, low-maintenance front yard xeriscape that slashes water usage, eliminates lawn mowing, and delivers long-lasting curb appeal.
