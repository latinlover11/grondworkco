Access for everyone

Accessibility statement

Groundwork Landscaping wants this website and our services to be usable by as many people as possible. We are continuing to improve the experience for visitors using different devices, browsers, and assistive technologies.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Our approach

We work toward an accessible experience by using semantic HTML, descriptive image alternatives, visible keyboard focus states, readable color contrast, labeled form controls, and reduced-motion preferences where animation is present.

Need help using the site?

If you have difficulty viewing content, navigating a page, completing the quote form, or accessing information in another format, contact us. We can help by phone at (720) 707-5411 or by email at cmoneyq11@gmail.com.

Report an access barrier

Please tell us the page or feature you were using, what went wrong, and the assistive technology or device involved if you know it. We will review the issue and look for a practical way to improve access.

For a direct response, use our contact form or email cmoneyq11@gmail.com.

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing responsibility rather than a one-time checklist. We will continue reviewing the site as content, technology, and our customers’ needs change.
