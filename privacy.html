Groundwork Landscaping respects your privacy. This notice explains what we collect when you contact us and how we use it to respond to your project request.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Information you provide

When you request a quote or contact us, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, service interest, timeline, budget, project details, and any photo link you choose to share.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to respond to your request, understand the project, schedule a consultation, provide an estimate, and communicate with you about that work. We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Forms and service providers

Quote forms are submitted through Formspree, a form-delivery service. Formspree receives the information needed to deliver your request to Groundwork Landscaping and may process it according to its own privacy terms. Please review Formspree’s privacy policy for details about that service.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use advertising cookies or a third-party analytics platform. Your browser may still retain ordinary technical data required to load and operate the site.
